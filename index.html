Biological Differentiation Lineages Reference

4 Target Phenotypes
Astrocyte

Star-shaped glial cells that form blood-brain barrier components, regulate neurotransmitter clearance, and provide structural support to CNS circuits.

NT-3 Induced

Stem cells differentiated with Neurotrophin-3 (NT-3) growth factor signaling, promoting sensory neuronal differentiation and neurite outgrowth elongation.

Oligodendrocyte

Myelin-producing cells of the central nervous system. Extend multi-branched lipid-rich processes that wrap around axons for saltatory signal conduction.

Retinoic Acid Induced

Differentiation induced by all-trans retinoic acid (ATRA), guiding neural progenitors toward mature spinal motor neuron and interneuron phenotypes.
